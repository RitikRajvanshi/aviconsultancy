Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you start your academic consultation with complete peace of mind.

Will my project/thesis be 100% free of plagiarism?

+
Yes, absolutely. We draft every paper from scratch based on primary or secondary empirical research. We run every submission through Turnitin and provide you with an authentic similarity report guaranteeing less than 10% similarity (strictly adhering to UGC norms).

How fast can you deliver an urgent MBA project?

+
Our standard delivery time is 7 to 10 days. However, for urgent deadlines, we have an express delivery track that can deliver a complete, high-quality project within 48 to 72 hours with dedicated priority research resources.

What if my university guide or professor requests modifications?

+
We provide free revisions until your guide gives final sign-off! Academic research is iterative; whenever your guide provides feedback on chapters, our team implements those modifications promptly at no additional charge.

Can you help with statistical software like SPSS, Excel, or AMOS?

+
Yes. Our statisticians handle quantitative hypothesis testing, Chi-Square tests, ANOVA, Regression, Factor Analysis, and Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) complete with data files, graphical charts, and analytical interpretations.

How do payment installments work?

+
We operate on a transparent, milestone-based structure: an initial booking advance to begin the outline/synopsis, followed by installments upon milestone drafts (Chapter 1-3 review, then final full draft delivery).
